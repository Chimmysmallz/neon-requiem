Neon Requiem

Privacy Notice

Effective 28 October 2026

Neon Requiem collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no cookies and no trackers.
  • Your progress and sound settings are saved only on your own phone or browser.
  • The game is built so it cannot contact any server.

Who makes the game

Neon Requiem is an independent game made by Chiamaka Nwabulue. If you have a question about this notice, write to [Add your contact email].

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for, collect or receive your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, advertising ID or any other personal information. There are no in-game purchases, no online accounts and no leaderboards.

What is stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game keeps a small amount of information on your own device:

StoredWhy
The district you reached and four story-choice countersSo the Continue button can resume your run and the story can reach the ending you earned.
Sound and music on or offSo your settings are remembered next time.

This stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

To delete it, choose any one of these:

  • Open the game, tap PRIVACY on the title screen, then ERASE SAVED DATA.
  • Finish the game. Your save is cleared automatically when you reach an ending.
  • In a browser, clear the site data for the page the game is on.
  • On Android, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

Phone permissions (Android app)

The Android version asks for one permission only: vibration, used for short buzzes when you are hit or land an attack. It does not request internet access or any permission that reads your data.

Where the game is hosted

If you play the web version, the page is served by GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub may automatically record technical information such as your IP address and browser type when the page loads. That is handled by GitHub, not by us, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or see this information.

If you download the game from an app store, the store handles your download under its own privacy policy.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Security

The game contains a built-in security policy that blocks it from loading anything from the internet or sending anything out. It has no third-party code, fonts or trackers.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about this notice or about your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 or any other privacy law, contact us at the address above.

Changes to this notice

If a future version adds anything that collects data, such as online leaderboards, we will update this notice before that version is released and ask for your permission where the law requires it. The date at the top shows when this notice last changed.
